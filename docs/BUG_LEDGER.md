# Bug ledger

The owner (2026-11-04): *"Let's keep doing what we can to keep track of and correct bugs."* This is the one list of bugs that were
FOUND, whether they are FIXED, what proves it, and what is still OPEN (found but not explained or not fixed). It starts at round 49d; the
earlier ones are in CLAUDE.md's status log and `docs/BOT_RUNS.md` (round 44's twelve).

Rules for this file:

- A bug is **fixed** only when a test (or a re-run of the thing that found it) fails without the fix. Say which.
- A bug is **open** when it was seen and not explained, or explained and not fixed. Say what was and was not shown. "Did not reproduce" is
  written as that, never as "fixed".
- A bug the owner's playtest finds goes in the same day it is reported; a bug the bot sweep finds goes in with the sweep's file name.
- Do not delete a row; move it to the FIXED table when it is.

## BOT SWEEP FINDINGS

Round 49n sweep (`docs/bot_runs/round49n_sweep.md`):

* **FIXED (bot, not the game) - the `none` bot sat "stuck" for 150 s in 7 of 18 playthroughs** (seeds 101 / 104 / 106; identical under the old rules, the ramp and the new rules, so it was not the ramp). The trace (`--trace`) showed `stam=0 hp=1`, the keys flipping left / right every ~2 s, and one standard zombie in state `attack` ~160 px away: biting a survivor in a performative fight that never ends. `bot_run._fight` counted any dead body in `chase / attack / hit` within 260 px as "coming for me", so a winded bot never rested; it kited (walked away 1.2 s, walked back) - and since round 49d **walking in combat mode stops stamina recovering**, so it could never get its wind back. Now a body only counts if it is after the bot (`_on_me`) or within 90 px. Seeds 101 / 104 / 106 re-run: no stuck, all three characters die or descend as before. Not a game fault; but it is the first place the round-49d "no recovery while walking in combat mode" rule bit something, noted in OPEN.
* **PROCESS (bot_sweep):** a playthrough killed by a container restart leaves a partial `<cfg>_<seed>.json` (two characters of three) which the resume rule treats as done - three seed-104 `need` runs were counted with 2 characters. **Fixed:** `bot_sweep.finished_run` now needs the log's "=== bot run done" line as well as the report; for this sweep the whole folder was re-run after the bot fix anyway.
* **FIXED (game) - the two `need_r0` `timeout`s (run 2 of seed 102, run 3 of seed 101, both floor 25, 150 s each) were the lift/body double answer above.** Both followed a previous character dying on floor 25, so the next one met its own body beside the merchant's lift; `NO_PROGRESS` listed `shop=open ... pack=open`. `need` and `need_r1` met no such body in their 6 playthroughs, so this was not a ramp effect - it just had not come up. The replay of those two seeds on the fixed tree (6 characters) came back clean - 0 timeouts, 0 anomalies - but the bot does not log meeting its own body, so that is absence of evidence; the proof is `floor15_test`. Details in `docs/bot_runs/round49n_sweep.md`.
* **Class sweep for "one [interact] press, two answers" (the bug's class) - now closed by `InteractArbiter` (see the FIXED row above).** Read every `is_action_just_pressed("interact")` site in `scripts/`. Arbitrated and tested with real nodes: lift (merchant + powered), flat door, maintenance door, the previous character's body, a loose drop, a stranger's `corridor_body`, the floor-30 backpack, the lobby door. Arbitrated and covered by the stub tests + the lint only (no real-node scenario): the survivor's [Talk] (`survivor_ai_test` still checks its older door pairing), the Tab-selected scavenge node (`room.gd` `_input`), the fuse box / workbench (`room.gd`; where their reaches meet the fuse box wins). **Not arbitrated, on purpose or still open:** `resident_npc.gd`'s trader [Trade] offer (read in `_unhandled_input` while its offer bubble is open, and it marks the press handled; `room.gd` reads the same press earlier in `_input`, so a trade offer made beside a scavenge node COULD be accepted and start a search with one press - I read this and did NOT reproduce or test it); `loot_ui.gd` (modal, held back by `loot_open`); `player.gd` / `hud.gd` only CANCEL things on the press. These are the next places to look if a sweep shows another `pack=open` / `shop=open` stall.
