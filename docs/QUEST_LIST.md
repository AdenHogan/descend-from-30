## Quest 001: Old Lady Ethel

A little old lady needs help with her husband. He's been bitten. You can give
her medical supplies hoping they can reach a cure in time, if there is one,
or put her husband out of his misery.

- **Outcome A (Provide Supplies):** Ethel is found dead and turned along with
  her husband in a later run.
  - *Reward:* The health kit is available to collect, but is half what it
    previously was when given to Ethel.
- **Outcome B (Kill Husband):** Ethel can be found in her home in a later
  run, still mourning her deceased husband. She is inconsolable. In the final
  run, Ethel will have killed herself.
  - *Reward:* Beside Ethel's body is a high-durability gun for the player to
    scavenge. Revolver with 5 of six bullets.

## Quest 002: The Shopkeeper

Multiple monsters are attacking the elevator doors on Floor 25. Use the
opportunity to sneak past them, or intervene and save the occupant inside
the elevator.

- **Outcome A (Save him → shopkeeper every 5th floor):** The shopkeeper
  becomes available to the player on all runs.
  - *Reward:* The Shop, featuring gear and upgrade paths.
- **Outcome B (No store or upgrades):** For a hard run (WITH ACHIEVEMENT) —
  on Runs 2 and 3 the player will find the shopkeeper dead on Floor 25. He
  has some items to scavenge, but access to the store on subsequent floors
  and the skill upgrade tree is locked.
  - *Reward:* A sense of accomplishment if you reach the lobby with a massive
    handicap, and some items to scavenge from the dead shopkeeper on Floor 25.

This is random: certain playthroughs the shopkeeper will just be available.
This encourages repeated playthroughs to ensure achievement success.

**RUN_DETAILS (from sheet):** Run 1 = NO, Run 2 = YES.

## Quest 003: The Babysitter

You hear a baby crying inside an apartment. Inside there are no adults, and
a choice must be made. Take the baby with you, or leave it be.

- **Outcome A (Take the baby):** Baby is equipped to the player, reducing
  stamina by half. Within an unknown number of descended floors (up to ten)
  the parent appears saying they got separated from their child. The parent
  takes back their child and player stamina returns to normal. In later runs,
  the player sees the parent holding a gun with the baby equipped, patrolling
  their apartment floor. This floor becomes a safe zone.
  - *Reward:* No physical reward, but subsequent runs turn the floor into a
    safe zone — no enemies, access to all apartments unlocked and scavenged
    items.
- **Outcome B (Leave the child):** Stamina is unaffected. The parent appears
  on another floor saying they must get back to their child. In subsequent
  runs, the player finds the parent, crazed, in their apartment. The child is
  nowhere to be seen. The player must either run or kill the parent, who
  attacks without warning. The parent will not leave the apartment to give
  chase, but killing the parent rewards the player with their weapon.
  - *Reward:* No reward for running; a legendary-durability weapon if the
    player kills the parent while hostile.

## Quest 004: Power the Elevator

Beside the elevator on a random floor, you find a maintenance worker. He says
the maniac shopkeeper took all the fuses that power the elevator and he
cannot find more. If the player brings three fuses, the maintenance worker
can get the elevator running.

- **Outcome A (Hunt for fuses):** While the quest is active, fuses have a
  slightly higher chance of appearing in scavenged rooms within three floors
  of the quest line, up and down. The worker stresses not to venture too far.
  Bring the fuses and he gets the elevator working for ten floors of
  descending travel.
  - *Reward:* Descend ten floors stress-free. Fuses for elevator travel
    become available.
- **Outcome B (Don't bring the fuses / go too far):** If the player can't
  find fuses, the worker can't get it running. If the player journeys beyond
  too many floors, returning finds the maintenance worker dead, surrounded by
  enemies.
  - *Reward:* The player can insert the fuses to travel four floors up or
    down. Fuses for elevator travel become available.

## Quest 005: The Prepper

You see a strangely locked door and hear movement coming from inside.
Knocking reveals a doomsday prepper who was ready for the end of the world
before it even started. She won't let you in unless you have something to
trade that she needs.

- **Outcome A (Find what she needs):** Bring the prepper the item and she
  opens her door, giving you a chance to heal up and resupply without
  resting.
  - *Reward:* Supplies and healing; safe rest spot without a building seed
    shift occurring.
- **Outcome B (Leave the prepper be):** Subsequent runs meet an even more
  untrusting prepper neighbour. She demands greater-value items in exchange
  for access to her apartment.
  - *Reward:* None — makes life harder for your subsequent runs.

## Quest 006: Into the Breach

You hear screams coming from a breached apartment. Inside, the enemies
aren't watching you, but slowly making their way to the back of the
apartment where a neighbour is desperately trying to survive.

- **Outcome A (Save the neighbour):** Defeat the enemies and boss in time and
  save your neighbour's life. The boss drops a key, and the neighbour rewards
  you.
  - *Reward:* Upgrade: Improved Hearing.
- **Outcome B (Don't save the neighbour):** The enemies reach the end of the
  apartment and kill the NPC, resulting in a loss of reward.
  - *Reward:* None.

## Quest 010: Johnny the Gun Guy

Bring Johnny ten bullets and he'll give you a good gun. Bring him more
bullets for an even greater reward.

- **Outcome A (Bring ten bullets):** Johnny gives the player a high-durability
  gun.
  - *Reward:* High-durability gun.
- **Outcome B (Don't give bullets):** Johnny stays around for subsequent runs
  but gives no rewards without a bullet exchange.
  - *Reward:* None.
- **Outcome C (Give more bullets):** For twenty bullets, Johnny rewards the
  player with an upgrade.
  - *Reward:* Upgrade: Bullet Refund OR Crit Shot Increase.
- **Outcome D (Don't give more bullets):** Johnny stays around for subsequent
  runs but gives no rewards without a bullet exchange.
  - *Reward:* None.
